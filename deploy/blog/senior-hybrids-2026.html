3. 시니어 골퍼를 위한 선택법

  • 어떤 아이언을 바꿀까: Golf Monthly는 200야드 이상 거리에서는 하이브리드가 롱아이언보다 더 관용적이고 효과적인 경우가 많다고 합니다. 3·4번부터 바꾸고, 5번 아이언도 띄우기 힘들면 5번까지 바꾸는 식이 무난합니다.
  • 로프트: 로프트는 제조사마다 조금씩 다릅니다. 숫자(3H·4H)보다 로프트 각도를 기준으로, 지금 가장 긴 아이언보다 거리가 한 클럽 더 나오면서 겹치지 않게 고르세요.
  • 샤프트: MyGolfSpy는 샤프트 강도가 스윙 속도와 맞아야 탄도가 맞는다고 설명합니다. 아이언을 스틸에서 그래파이트로 바꿀 시기라면 하이브리드도 가벼운 그래파이트·시니어(A) 플렉스를 함께 고려하세요.
  • 스핀: 원래 공이 높이 뜨고 스핀이 많은 분은 Mizuno JPX One이나 Wilson DYNAPWR처럼 스핀이 많은 모델이 오히려 거리를 줄일 수 있습니다.

When swing speed drops, the first clubs to get hard are the 3-, 4- and 5-irons. The ball doesn't climb enough to hold greens, and small mishits cost a lot of distance. That's why so many senior golfers swap long irons for hybrids. MyGolfSpy puts it simply: "Hybrids are meant as long-iron replacements," and some golfers may want to replace irons up to a 5- or 6-iron. The biggest advantage over an iron is the potential for better launch conditions.

The timing is good: MyGolfSpy recently reported that the Mizuno JPX One finished first overall in both its 2026 hybrid and fairway wood tests, a surprise sweep for a brand many Korean-American golfers already trust. Below we combine MyGolfSpy's test data with senior-focused hybrid reviews from Golf Monthly and National Club Golfer (NCG) to find the best fits for slower swings.

3. How seniors should choose

  • Which irons to replace: Golf Monthly says that from around 200 yards and beyond, a hybrid is often more forgiving and effective than a long iron. Start with the 3- and 4-iron, and add the 5 if it's hard to launch.
  • Loft: Lofts vary by maker. Choose by loft angle, not the number on the sole, so the hybrid goes one club longer than your longest iron without overlapping.
  • Shaft: MyGolfSpy notes that flex should match swing speed to get the right flight. If you're moving from steel to graphite irons, consider a light graphite senior (A) flex in your hybrids too.
  • Spin: If you already launch it high with lots of spin, high-spin heads like the Mizuno JPX One or Wilson DYNAPWR may cost you distance.
